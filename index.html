CYD Phone Notification Display — mirrored from your phone via Pushbullet

📲 Phone Notifications 🇬🇦 Arabic Supported 🕑 12-Hour Clock ⚡ Instant Delivery
1. Connect the CYD / ESP32 board to your computer with a USB cable
2. Press the Install button below and choose the serial port
3. When flashing finishes, the device broadcasts a Wi-Fi network named CYD-Setup
4. Join it from your phone and open 192.168.4.1 (the page usually opens by itself)
5. Pick your home Wi-Fi, enter the password, paste your Pushbullet Access Token, then Save
6. The device restarts and starts showing your phone notifications, in English and Arabic ✅
Wiring for standalone display:
Display CS → GPIO 15  |  DC → GPIO 2  |  RESET → EN
MOSI → GPIO 13  |  SCK → GPIO 14  |  MISO → GPIO 12 (optional)
Backlight LED → GPIO 21 (or 3.3V)  |  VCC → 3.3V  |  GND → GND
Touch (optional): CLK → 25, CS → 33, DIN → 32, DO → 39
Your browser does not support web-based flashing. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or Android.

Installing requires Chrome or Edge (desktop or Android). The setup steps after install work from any phone, including iPhone. Find your Pushbullet token at: pushbullet.com → Settings → Access Tokens → Create Access Token (it starts with o.). To change Wi-Fi or the token later: open the device address from the same network. If the saved Wi-Fi is unreachable, the device automatically broadcasts CYD-Setup again. Touch guide: tap the left third of the screen for the previous notification, the right third for the next one, and the middle to mark all as read.
